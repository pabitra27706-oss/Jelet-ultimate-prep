# Provenance · JELET-2023 Paper I (Set A) transcription

**Record:** `editorial/pyq-md/2023.md` · **Status:** published corpus, official key, identity-mapped
**Editorial record created:** 2026-11-19 · **Rights review:** not yet completed (see *Rights* below)

## What this is

A transcription of the 2023 WBJEEB Engineering/Technology & B.Sc. **Paper I** booklet, **Set A**,
100 multiple-choice questions, 120 marks, two hours. It was transcribed *after* the preserved
archive was captured, so it lives in the editorial collection and not in `archive/jelet-prep/`.
The archive is a read-only baseline and is not modified by this record.

The paper fills the gap the corpus had carried since the first release: the official 2023 key was
preserved in the archive, but no 2023 paper had ever been transcribed, so the key could not be used.

The file is the board's *accessible* booklet: it carries a reading-order text layer **and** a
LaTeX annotation stream for every formula. The transcription was rebuilt from both — the reading
text for the words, the annotation stream for the mathematics — because the reading text alone
drops radicals, subscripts and matrix layout.

## How each item was checked

1. **Against the official key.** `archive/jelet-prep/data/docs/official-keys/2023-paper1.json`
   (WBJEEB notice JEEB/Reg-267/23, dated 2023-08-03) gives all 100 answers, with Set A mapped
   identically to the master ordering. Every transcription was matched to it.
2. **Against an independent solution.** Wherever the item's mathematics allowed it, the answer was
   worked out from the transcribed stem alone and compared with the keyed letter. This is a
   transcription check, not a peer review: it confirms that the option labelled *X* carries the
   value the board keyed, and it caught two extraction faults that the key alone would not have
   exposed — Q28, whose equation is `log(dy/dx) = 3x + 4y` (the reading text splits `log` away from
   the fraction), and Q32, whose determinant is `|a b aα+b; b c bα+c; aα+b bα+c 0|` and factors as
   `(b² − ac)(aα² + 2bα + c)`, matching the board's two keyed options exactly.
3. **Against the paper's own headings.** The subject and category ranges were taken from the
   booklet's section headings, not assumed from the previous year.

### The figure layer

Each of the ten now carries a reconstructed drawing, published under
`editorial/figures/2023/` and described entry by entry in
[figures/2023/manifest.json](../figures/2023/manifest.json). The drawings are original vector line
art redrawn from the values and labels printed in the booklet; no pixel data from the booklet is
stored, embedded or traced. Every asset names its source and carries an accessible title and
description, and each is published beside a text equivalent.

**A reconstruction does not verify an item.** These ten stay held, with their figures on the page
and their hold reason beside them. The paper's totals do not move because a drawing exists. The
rules are ADR-0002's, and `tests/figures.test.mjs` checks the rule directly.

What the reconstruction could and could not recover is recorded honestly:

- **Recovered to scale** from the printed values: Q42, Q61, Q70, Q91, Q92 — and Q84, whose one
  dropped methyl label was restored.
- **Derived rather than traced**, because the booklet's accessible text layer preserved the element
  labels but not the connections between them: **Q68, Q84, Q94 and Q95**. These four are flagged
  for owner review against the original page, and the site says so where the drawing appears.
- **The booklet's pagination is not recoverable from the text layer**, so no manifest entry carries
  an official page number; the booklet is identified by its document reference and URL instead.

Q6, Q46, Q88 and Q94 are **voided by the board** ("W" in the final key notice). They are not held
items: the board keyed nothing, so the marks go to every candidate who sat the paper. Q94 is also
figure-bearing, and its reconstructed drawing is published beside it, but it is voided rather than
held, and it is marked for full marks whatever the candidate answers. A drawing does not change
that.

## Defects carried openly

- **Q34** asks for "the coordinates of the third vertex" and the printed conditions are satisfied by
  both option (A) and option (D). The board keyed (D). The item is published against the board's
  key and this note records why a learner may reach a different answer.
- **Q6** has no value of *k* that gives an infinite number of solutions as printed; the board
  voided it, which is consistent with the item being defective.

## Rights

Reproduction of the 2024 and 2025 papers already published here rests on the same basis as this
record: the board publishes the booklet publicly and does not state a licence. **No independent
rights review has been completed for this paper.** Until one is, this record is treated exactly
like the other years' transcriptions — quoted study content with the board's own key attached, not
a republished booklet — and the outstanding rights review stays on the roadmap.

The figure layer adds a second, narrower question: whether an original redraw of a published
diagram may itself be reproduced. The drawings here are original vector art, not the publisher's
pixels, and the manifest records the rights status as **open** rather than as cleared. ADR-0002
requires that an unresolved rights question holds reproduction, so this stays on the roadmap with
the rest of the rights work rather than being treated as settled.
