**Second unreleased increment (2026-11-19, same branch):** the 2023 Paper I Set A paper, transcribed into the editorial collection from the official booklet, and the figure layer that goes with it.

- **The paper.** 100 questions transcribed, 86 scoreable, 4 voided by the board and awarded to every candidate, 10 held, 90 rehearsable. The official key is the board's own notice JEEB/Reg-267/23, Set A, identity-mapped. The corpus is now 790 questions over eight papers.
- **The figure layer.** Eleven reconstructed SVG drawings for the items whose stems point at a diagram, published under `public/figures/2023/`, inlined into the reader so they follow the page theme, and always shipped with a text equivalent and the statement that they are reconstructions. Built to ADR-0002: original vector primitives, explicit provenance, no official pixels, and **no automatic scoring** — the ten held items stay held and the paper's totals do not move.
- **What it does not settle.** The booklet's accessible text layer preserved the printed values and labels but not its artwork, so four of the eleven figures — Q68, Q84, Q94 and Q95 — have derived wiring or layout and are flagged for owner review against the original page. The rights status of redrawing a published diagram remains open and is recorded in the editorial manifest.
