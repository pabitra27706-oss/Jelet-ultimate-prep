- **Approved scope, delivered outputs, deliberate exclusions:** the Learn route
  only. Delivered:
  1. **Title and promise** — an `h1` “Learn”, “Build your concepts. Ace your
     future.”, and a truthful sentence that states the real totals (4 subjects,
     42 topic groups, 1 written lesson) and where the rest of the material is.
  2. **Search** — a labelled search field over subjects, topic names, lesson
     titles and topic descriptions, which filters the already-rendered list (no
     request, no new index) and reports “N of 42 topics shown for …”. A **Clear**
     control appears with text, and the zero-result state names the problem and
     offers “Show the whole syllabus”.
  3. **Lesson filter** — three pressed-state chips (“All topics”, “With a
     lesson”, “Notes only”) that narrow the same list and keep `aria-pressed`
     honest. With one authored topic, “With a lesson” returns exactly one.
  4. **Four subject cards** — Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Electrical &
     Electronics Engineering, each with a subject-coloured spine and icon tile,
     the true sentence (“1 of 8 topics has a written lesson” / “No written
     lesson yet · 15 topics of notes and past questions”) and a meter labelled
     `aria-label="… written lessons"` whose value is that count, not a course
     completion. Each card routes to its own ordered topic section.
  5. **Ordered topics** — all 42 syllabus topics in teaching order, each with
     its real state: a lesson tag and minutes with a link to the lesson, or
     “Notes only” with a link to the topic's anchor on the revision-notes page.
     No topic is hidden and none is invented.
  Excluded: audio lessons, AI tutor, chapter totals, completion percentages,
  invented counts, new syllabus evidence and any new dependency.
