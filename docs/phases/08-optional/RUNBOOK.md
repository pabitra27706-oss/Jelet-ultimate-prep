# 08 field guide · three independent decisions, not a feature bundle

**08.1 Need and cost brief.** For *one* candidate, record the student problem, evidence, existing free workaround, current provider terms/date, caps on compute/storage/egress and what happens at exhaustion. A trial, a budget alert without a hard stop or a required billing card fails the project rule. If the owner declines, record no-go and stop before provisioning anything.

**08.2 Sync experiment (only if approved).** Outline guest-to-account merge conflicts, isolated user reads/writes, exports, deletion, error and outage flows. Verify with synthetic accounts and no private exports. Switching user must not expose the previous person's notes. Provider off → lessons and manual backup still work. Failed isolation or uncertain quota: disable sync, keep guest data intact.

**08.3 Tutor experiment (separate approval).** Compare reviewed hint ladder against the unmet need first. If still justified, require explicit opt-in, minimal context, secrets outside static output, strict no-bill cutoff and no responses during rehearsal. Verify on wrong-key suggestions, quota exhaustion and offline use: official grading must not change. If hard limits cannot be guaranteed, keep reviewed hints or the previewed copyable prompt only.

**08.4 Pharmacy scoping (no dependency on either service).** Request the actual Paper II syllabus, booklets, rights and edition rules. Build its own inventory and pilot audit. Do not rebrand Paper I tests; reject shared defaults that assume Paper I categories. Return to the evidence phase with a separately approved scope.

**Output per candidate:** signed-off decision or clearly pending owner decision, evidence links/date, maximum owner liability, tested fallback, shutdown procedure and unresolved risks. “Not needed” is a successful decision. No deployment, signup or vendor commitment belongs in the feasibility report.
