**Counts to confirm at pre-flight, not to assume:** 100 questions, 10 figure-bearing,
3 incomplete-option, complete-option and figure-free ceiling **89**. These are
starting expectations from the corpus; the booklet is what counts.

**Confirmed by the 2026-11-20 pre-flight, from the booklet's text layer only:** 100
numbered items are present with no gap, and ten stems name a figure — the same ten the
corpus flags. The 89 ceiling stays an *expectation*: figure status and option

corpus stem belongs to a different question (Q18). None of those three is counted in
the ceiling until the page settles it.

### Text-layer quirks to expect

The 2017 text layer interleaves two-column option blocks with their own stems. Q81,
Q82, Q84 and Q97 come out with the options split across lines and mixed with the
stem text. That is an artefact of extraction, not a printing error. **Read the page
image.** Q97's "options" are diagrams, and the extracted "(A) 15 kN/m 20 kN 10 kN/m
2 m 1 m 4 m 2 m 76 kN 44 kN" is the diagram's data, not an option.

## 4 · Procedure

Follow the [runbook](../RUNBOOK.md) in order. Year-specific notes:

**09.1 Pre-flight** — confirm the section map above against the booklet and record
the printed page on which each section begins. 2017 has three Q71–100 sections; note
where each one starts so the main set is unambiguous.

**09.2 Page capture** — the booklet is short enough to capture whole. Index by
printed page. **This step is where the 2026-11-20 session stopped:** the sandbox
cannot render the booklet (see the [pre-flight record](../../../../editorial/derived-keys/2017/2017-preflight.md)
§8), so a run needs the owner's page images before it can begin.

**09.3 Reconciliation** — expect 45 subject-label conflicts, one unusable
option set (Q83), and check whether any other corpus stem or option differs from the
booklet wording. Note especially any stem the corpus truncated: the legacy
transcription frequently drops the tail of a sentence.

**09.4 First pass** — Q21–35 Electrical Technology is the most mechanical block after
the Mathematics section; Q36–55 Computer Application is largely factual and checks
cleanly; Q71–85 Engineering Mechanics and Q86–100 Strength of Materials carry most of
the figures. Every item is Category-I, so adjudicating four options per item is the
rule, not a Category-II special case.

**09.5 Attack** — spend the attack budget on the Strength of Materials items: 2017's
Q96–99 are numerical and the distractors are close together.

**09.6 Cross-check** — a second solver on the page images, with no access to the
first pass.

**09.7 Machine checks** — 2017 rewards them: the statics items (Q71–85) and the
strength-of-materials items (Q86–100) are directly computable. Write small scripts
for the numeric ones and keep them in `checks/`.

**09.8 Adjudication** — tier every item. `official` is unavailable for this year.

**09.9 Publish** — write the four artifacts, then stop and hand the owner a summary.

## 5 · Tier semantics

The four tiers are defined in the [phase README](../README.md#two-independent-axes-trust-and-eligibility).
For 2017, in one line: **`official` does not exist in practice.** No key for 2017 was found — nothing in the Board's key archive (checked
2026-11-20), which is evidence of absence, not proof of non-publication — so no
item may carry that tier. Everything is `derived-verified`,
`derived-provisional` or `disputed`, and only `derived-verified` may ever be scored —
and only after the owner says so and the two integration gates are cleared.

2017-specific expectation: Q83 and the two diagram-option items (Q97, Q100) are
almost certainly `disputed` or held. That is the correct outcome, not a shortfall.

## 6 · Stop conditions

The full table is in [STOP-CONDITIONS.md](../STOP-CONDITIONS.md). The ones most
likely to fire for 2017:

- **S3 — options cannot be read.** Applies to Q83 if the page image does not resolve
  the four exponents. If it does not resolve, the item is `disputed` with
  `options-not-reproduced`. **Never write the "standard formula" into the option
  text** — that is precisely the defect this phase exists to fix.
- **S4 — corpus and booklet disagree.** 2017 will produce many. The booklet wins.
- **S9 — a section is mostly holes.** If Strength of Materials comes out mostly
  held, stop that section, finish the rest, and escalate to the owner. Do not
  publish a key whose back half is unreadable without saying so loudly.

## 8 · Owner practice loop

The owner approves the key, the paper is integrated in a later phase, the owner
practises it on the site and reports what looks wrong. Each report is traced to the
evidence for that item and adjudicated against the page. The loop is described in
[OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md](../OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md) and works identically for all
four years. 2017-specific note: the owner is likely to hit **Q83** and the two
diagram-option items first — those are exactly the items where the site should say
"this one is held because the options were not reproduced", and that message must be
readable on a phone.
