### Text-layer quirks to expect

The 2018 text layer is generally clean. Two things to watch:

- **Q89 and Q91 come out as bare `(A) (B) (C) (D)`** with no text. That is the
  extractor seeing four images and printing the labels. It does **not** mean the
  options are missing — it means the options *are* images. The items are
  figure-dependent, not option-less.
- The booklet prints two options per line in the Computer Application section, so
  Q45–Q50's options arrive in pairs. Read the page image.
- Page numbers survive on only five pages (7, 11, 13, 15, 27), so the page map in the
  pre-flight record is anchored on those and inferred between them.
- Printed page 27 carries the end of the Printing set and the start of the
  Agricultural set, so a page number never identifies the stream set on its own.

## 4 · Procedure

Follow the [runbook](../RUNBOOK.md) in order. Year-specific notes:

**09.1 Pre-flight** — confirm the section map above. 2018 has three Q71–100
sections; note where each starts so the JT3 set is unambiguous. Read the booklet's
cover page for the booklet number.

**09.2 Page capture** — index by printed page.

**09.3 Reconciliation** — expect 45 subject-label conflicts, the Q72 stem
truncation, and the three substituted Mathematics stems below. Check for others: the legacy transcription drops sentence tails in
several places.

**09.4 First pass** — Mathematics (Q1–20) is the block most damaged in the corpus, so
solve it from the page and reconcile before trusting any corpus stem. Q21–35
Electrical Technology is the most mechanical block; Q36–55 Computer Application is
factual and checks cleanly; Q71–85 Engineering Mechanics and Q86–100 Strength of
Materials carry most of the figures. Every item is Category-I, so adjudicating four
options per item is the rule, not a Category-II special case.

**09.5 Attack** — spend the attack budget on Q86–100. Q88, Q95, Q96 and Q97 have
closely-spaced distractors that reward an explicit substitution check.

**09.6 Cross-check** — a second solver on the page images, with no access to the
first pass.

**09.7 Machine checks** — 2018 rewards them: Q72's moment balance, Q85's sphere
geometry, Q92 and Q94's stress transforms, Q96 and Q99's energy relations are all
directly computable. Keep the scripts in `checks/`.

**09.8 Adjudication** — tier every item. `official` is unavailable for this year.

**09.9 Publish** — write the four artifacts, then stop and hand the owner a summary.

## 5 · Tier semantics

The four tiers are defined in the [phase README](../README.md#two-independent-axes-trust-and-eligibility).
For 2018, in one line: **`official` does not exist.** The board published no key for
2018, so no item may carry that tier. Everything is `derived-verified`,
`derived-provisional` or `disputed`, and only `derived-verified` may ever be scored —
and only after the owner says so and the two integration gates are cleared.

2018-specific expectation: Q89 and Q91 are almost certainly held. That is the
correct outcome, not a shortfall.

## 6 · Stop conditions

The full table is in [STOP-CONDITIONS.md](../STOP-CONDITIONS.md). The ones most
likely to fire for 2018:

- **S3 — options cannot be read.** Applies if the diagram options at Q89 or Q91
  cannot be resolved from the page image. Then the item is `disputed` with
  `options-not-reproduced` and `optionFidelity: "figure-based"`. **Never describe a
  diagram in the option text** — a written description of an unreproduced diagram is
  an invention, and the reconstructed figure layer exists precisely so that drawings
  live in `editorial/figures/<year>/` under ADR-0002, with a text equivalent and a
  statement of how the redraw differs.
- **S4 — corpus and booklet disagree.** 2018 will produce many. The booklet wins.
- **S9 — a section is mostly holes.** If Strength of Materials comes out mostly
  held, stop that section, finish the rest, and escalate to the owner.

## 8 · Owner practice loop

The owner approves the key, the paper is integrated in a later phase, the owner
practises it on the site and reports what looks wrong. Each report is traced to the
evidence for that item and adjudicated against the page. The loop is described in
[OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md](../OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md) and works identically for all
four years. 2018-specific note: the owner is likely to meet **Q89** and **Q91**
early — the site must say "this one is held because the options are diagrams we
could not reproduce", and that message must be readable on a phone.
