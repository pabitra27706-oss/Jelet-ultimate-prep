## 4 · Procedure

Follow the [runbook](../RUNBOOK.md) in order. Year-specific notes:

**09.1 Pre-flight** — confirm the section map above and settle the alternate-set
question. Read the whole booklet once before solving anything. Because two questions
are absent from the corpus and one stem is blank, the booklet is the only complete
picture; do not let the corpus's 98-item shape bias the reading.

**09.2 Page capture** — index by printed page. Q77's page is mandatory: it is the
evidence for the blank-stem finding.

**09.3 Reconciliation** — expect the two missing questions, the blank Q77 stem, the
subject-label conflicts, and possibly option or stem differences elsewhere. The
legacy 2019 transcription's wrong year heading is itself a record: note that the
corpus for 2019 was derived from a file labelled 2018, and check whether any 2019
corpus record is actually a 2018 question. This is worth a deliberate pass: it is the
cheapest way to find a transposition error.

**09.4 First pass** — 100 items, including Q78 and Q79, from the page images.

**09.5 Attack** — spend the attack budget on Q71–85 Engineering Mechanics, where the
figures and the blank stem both live.

**09.6 Cross-check** — a second solver on the page images, with no access to the
first pass. Give the second solver the same 100 items, not the corpus's 98, so that
Q78 and Q79 are cross-checked too.

**09.7 Machine checks** — Q71–85 and Q86–100 are directly computable. Write the
scripts.

**09.8 Adjudication** — tier every item. `official` is unavailable for this year.
Expect Q77 to be `disputed` and held; that is a finding, not a failure.

**09.9 Publish** — write the four artifacts, then stop and hand the owner a summary.

## 5 · Tier semantics

The four tiers are defined in the [phase README](../README.md#two-independent-axes-trust-and-eligibility).
For 2019, in one line: **`official` does not exist.** The board published no key for
2019, so no item may carry that tier. Everything is `derived-verified`,
`derived-provisional` or `disputed`, and only `derived-verified` may ever be scored —
and only after the owner says so and the two integration gates are cleared.

Because 2019 has the most known defects, it is also the year most likely to produce a
large `disputed` count. That is acceptable and expected. A 2019 key that reports 80
verified, 10 provisional and 10 disputed is more useful than one that reports 100
verified with no evidence.

## 6 · Stop conditions

The full table is in [STOP-CONDITIONS.md](../STOP-CONDITIONS.md). The ones most
likely to fire for 2019:

- **S6 — a printed stem is blank.** Q77 is the known case. Re-read the page, then
  record it as `disputed` with `input-unreadable` and an open dispute. **Never
  reconstruct the question.** The surrounding questions are about the angular
  momentum of a body on a rough surface; reconstructing a plausible stem from that
  context would be inventing an exam question, and it is the single most likely
  place for a phase to cheat.
- **S5 — a question is in the booklet but not in the corpus.** Q78 and Q79 are the
  known cases. Solve them normally; record them in `missingInCorpus`.
- **S4 — corpus and booklet disagree.** 2019 will produce many. The booklet wins.
- **S8 — the two solvers disagree on more than a third of the items.** For 2019 this
  has an obvious suspect: a transcription year-label error. Re-check the input
  before assuming the solvers are at fault.
- **S9 — a section is mostly holes.** If Engineering Mechanics comes out mostly
  held, stop that section, finish the rest, and escalate to the owner.

## 8 · Owner practice loop

The owner approves the key, the paper is integrated in a later phase, the owner
practises it on the site and reports what looks wrong. Each report is traced to the
evidence for that item and adjudicated against the page. The loop is described in
[OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md](../OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md) and works identically for all
four years. 2019-specific note: the owner will meet **Q77** almost immediately, and
the site must handle it gracefully — an item that the board printed without a stem
should read as "the booklet prints no question here", not as a broken page or a zero
score that looks like the owner's fault.
