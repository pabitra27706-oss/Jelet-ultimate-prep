### Text-layer quirks to expect — the important one

**The 2021 PDF text layer drops visible question labels around Q74, Q76 and Q81.**
The questions themselves are present; their numbers are not. This produces two
traps:

1. Do **not** conclude that a question is missing because its label is missing. The
   question is there; the label did not survive extraction.
2. Do **not** renumber by inference. Number the questions from the page images,
   where the labels are printed, and record the label as missing in the text layer
   only.

The 2021 text layer also loses artwork entirely, so every diagram-bearing item reads
as an option list with missing entries. "The text layer shows no options here" means
"the options are images until proven otherwise".

## 4 · Procedure

Follow the [runbook](../RUNBOOK.md) in order. Year-specific notes:

**09.1 Pre-flight** — confirm the section map above and the alternate-set list.

**09.2 Page capture** — index by printed page, and **number the questions from the
page images**. The text layer's dropped labels make any other numbering unsafe.

**09.3 Reconciliation** — expect the subject-label conflicts, plus a specific check
on Q74, Q76 and Q81: confirm each is present in the booklet, correctly numbered, and
that the corpus record matches the booklet text. Also check whether the corpus's
stem for any of them was aligned to the *text layer's* numbering rather than the
page's — if so, the corpus may have them numbered correctly but the wrong content,
or the wrong content correctly numbered. Read the page.

**09.4 First pass** — 100 items from the page images, with the figure status
recorded for every one.

**09.5 Attack** — spend the attack budget on the Strength of Materials items
(Q89–Q99), where the distractors are close and the figures decide the reading.

**09.6 Cross-check** — a second solver on the page images, with no access to the
first pass. Because this year has the most figures, the second solver's ability to
read the *same* figures is the point of the exercise. A solver that cannot read a
figure must return `UNDETERMINED`, not a plausible guess.

**09.7 Machine checks** — the Engineering Mechanics and Strength of Materials items
are directly computable. Write the scripts; they are also the way to settle an item
whose answer is right but whose figure cannot be reproduced for a reader.

**09.8 Adjudication** — tier every item. `official` is unavailable for this year.

**09.9 Publish** — write the four artifacts, then stop and hand the owner a summary.

## 5 · Tier semantics

The four tiers are defined in the [phase README](../README.md#two-independent-axes-trust-and-eligibility).
For 2021, in one line: **`official` does not exist.** The board published no key for
2021, so no item may carry that tier. Everything is `derived-verified`,
`derived-provisional` or `disputed`, and only `derived-verified` may ever be scored —
and only after the owner says so and the two integration gates are cleared.

2021-specific expectation: this year will produce the **highest** held count of the
four. That is a property of the paper, not of the solving. A 2021 key with 70
verified, 15 provisional and 15 held is a good key.

## 6 · Stop conditions

The full table is in [STOP-CONDITIONS.md](../STOP-CONDITIONS.md). The ones most
likely to fire for 2021:

- **S2 — a page image is unreadable.** With 15 figure items this is the most likely
  stop in the phase. Record the item as `disputed` with `input-unreadable`, or hold
  it with a solved answer if the answer could be determined from the text. **Never
  infer the geometry.** A wrong figure produces a confidently wrong key, which is
  worse than an admitted gap.
- **S3 — options cannot be read.** Applies to Q26 and Q35 if their option sets cannot
  be resolved. Then `options-not-reproduced` and `disputed`.
- **S4 — corpus and booklet disagree.** 2021 will produce many. The booklet wins.
  Pay particular attention to Q74, Q76 and Q81.
- **S9 — a section is mostly holes.** If Strength of Materials comes out mostly
  held, stop that section, finish the rest, and escalate to the owner rather than
  publishing a key whose back half is unreadable.

**Owner review (separate, ongoing):** approve or refuse scoring; practise; report;
approve each correction.

## 8 · Owner practice loop

The owner approves the key, the paper is integrated in a later phase, the owner
practises it on the site and reports what looks wrong. Each report is traced to the
evidence for that item and adjudicated against the page. The loop is described in
[OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md](../OWNER-PRACTICE-LOOP.md) and works identically for all
four years. 2021-specific note: this is the year the owner will most often hit a
held item, because it has the most figures. The site's message has to be plain and
must not read as a failure of the owner's own work.
