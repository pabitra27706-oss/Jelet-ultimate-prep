- **Approved scope, delivered outputs, deliberate exclusions:** the Today route
  only; the older hero was replaced, everything below it kept. Delivered:
  1. **Brand and promise** — an in-page lockup “JELET **Ultimate Prep**” with
     “Smarter Study. Brighter Tomorrow.”, and a real link to You in place of the
     reference's avatar and bell. The app bar's duplicate brand text is hidden
     on this route only.
  2. **One teal panel** — a real “START HERE” or “CONTINUE” eyebrow, the current
     activity, a truthful copy line, a **real meter** (the saved lesson section
     out of the six, or answered items out of the attempt size, with a text
     label and `aria-valuenow`), one primary action, and a disclosure holding
     the three older routes. A first visit shows the actual Parallel Circuits
     lesson and an honest “Start the lesson”; a returning reader sees “Welcome
     back to your lesson.” and the exact `?resume=1#<section>` link, and never
     a fabricated progress figure.
  3. **Three quick actions** — Learn, Papers and the mock builder, each a live
     route with its icon; compact tiles on the phone, full labels on desktop.
  4. **Review tile** — the real due count and a sentence that distinguishes due
     today from items scheduled later, and names the empty queue honestly.
  5. **Four subject cards** — two columns on the phone, four on desktop, each
     showing the real “N / M questions answered” over the exact eligible 2025
     drill IDs, a meter and a link to that drill. The wording is “questions
     answered”, never “mastered” or a course percentage.
  6. **A plan card** — “Small steps add up.” to the real plan, with no streak or
     efficacy promise.
  7. **Everything preserved below** — the record panel with its four real
     statistics, the resume block, the existing papers list and the
     sources/scoring/privacy disclosure.
  Excluded: AI, formula sheets, daily challenges, timers, notifications, avatars
  and percentages; no new dependency.
