## Item findings and topic candidates

All topic IDs refer to the [2026 syllabus inventory](inventory.md) as **current teaching candidates**, not a claim about official 2025 topic placement. No reviewed lesson section exists, so none has an exact section link or published primary/secondary lesson mapping. Checks of reasoning below were done by the agent separately from reading the key; no independently reviewed worked explanation exists. For all ten: source attribution is WBJEEB; **reproduction permission is unresolved** for question text/options/figures, public modes are **none**, and applicable evidence is link-only. The [legacy rights record](../../../archive/jelet-prep/data/docs/LICENSING.md) does not grant permission. Item-specific limitations follow.

- **Q5 — Mathematics, `jelet-syllabus:mathematics:t08` (primary candidate: homogeneous functions and partial derivatives).** A–D expressions align in order; no figure referenced. The Board extraction shows powers 7 in the numerator, the legacy text uses powers 2. A degree-six quotient yields the keyed coefficient by the chain rule; the legacy quotient is degree one and does not. Hold for page-image confirmation of the exponents and correction in a new editorial record, not in the archive.
- **Q7 — Mathematics, `jelet-syllabus:mathematics:t06` (primary: removable discontinuities).** Piecewise rule and all four options align in the available text; no figure. Factoring the numerator leaves a limit of `3 − A`; equating to the assigned value 5 gives −2, consistent with D. No detected text defect; rights and review unresolved.
- **Q10 — Mathematics, `jelet-syllabus:mathematics:t10` (primary: first-order equations and substitution).** Stem aligns; A aligns; Board-extracted B has the coefficient 2 (legacy D), C has coefficient 4 (legacy B), and Board D uses the inverse ratio, absent from the legacy options. Legacy C is not among the extracted options. No figure. Substituting `y = xz` for nonzero `x` gives `eˣ dx + 4z dz = 0`, consistent with Board B, not legacy B. Hold for page-image option review and correction; do not key the legacy row as-is.
- **Q32 — Mathematics, `jelet-syllabus:mathematics:t06` (primary: continuity versus differentiability).** Case definition and options A–D align in extracted text; no figure. The limit at zero and the difference quotient both tend to zero, whereas the off-zero derivative has an oscillatory term that does not tend to zero. This supports B, C and D, not A. Rights and review unresolved.
- **Q41 — Physics, `jelet-syllabus:physics:t01` (primary: dimensions of terms in a sum).** The local stem abridges the definitions of pressure, volume and temperature; options B–D align with the extraction, and both show an unusual A expression containing repeated time symbols. Exact visual exponent/letter not checked; no figure. Record `W` without guessing an answer or the Board’s reason; exclude from ordinary practice scoring. The Board’s full-exam withdrawal instruction is not a key letter.
- **Q44 — Physics, `jelet-syllabus:physics:t02` (primary: acceleration and velocity-time graphs).** Extracted stem gives initial velocity zero, consistent with the local summary. The transcription omits the input graph and all four graphical choices; the PDF extraction exposes A–D labels but does not supply the plots. Axis labels, scale, line shapes, visual order and accessible descriptions are unverified. No independent answer check; keep the official C as a notice fact only. Need the actual printed p. 11 graph and choices.
- **Q71 — Chemistry, `jelet-syllabus:chemistry:t01` (primary: quantum numbers and subshell labels).** Both quantum numbers and the A–D order align in text; no figure. The `l = 3` subshell is f, hence 5f (D). Rights and review unresolved.
- **Q77 — Chemistry, `jelet-syllabus:chemistry:t05` (primary: Le Chatelier’s principle and compression).** Text comparison matches reactions, phase labels and order A–D; no figure. In C, the forward reaction consumes gas without producing a gaseous product; the other choices do not reduce gaseous mole count going forward. Consistent with C. This adds a practice lead for the group left unchecked in 01.1; it is not a lesson or clearance.
- **Q92 — Electrical & Electronics, `jelet-syllabus:electrical-and-electronics:t02` (primary: ideal transformer input/output power).** Supply, load and option order align; decimal dot typography differs without changing values. No figure. Ideal power transfer with the resistive load gives `100 W / 200 V = 0.5 A`, consistent with B. Rights and review unresolved.
- **Q95 — Electrical & Electronics, `jelet-syllabus:electrical-and-electronics:t05` (primary: AC sources and circuits, phase).** Formula options A–D align in extracted text. Board text names `I`, `I₁` and `I₂`; legacy uses `I₁`, `I₂` and `I₃` and omits the circuit. The figure is not exposed in the PDF text extraction. Connections, labels and any description cannot be verified. No independent answer check; treat B only as the Board’s key. Need printed p. 25 with diagram. This is an **AC** question, not an established link to the planned DC parallel-resistance lesson.
