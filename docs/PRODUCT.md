# Student experience

**Scope:** Paper I (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Fundamentals of Electrical & Electronics Engineering). A historical library is not a promise of modern mock coverage. Pharmacy needs a separate curriculum, corpus and scoring review. Independent preparation resource, not a board service.

| Destination | First visit | Returning student |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Choose a subject and goal without an onboarding form | One primary action: resume the exact lesson/attempt or retry due questions |
| Learn | Browse subject → chapter → lesson; see prerequisites and availability | Continue a specific example; revisit a saved step or revision sheet |
| Practice | Choose guided, independent drill, reader or eligible rehearsal | Resume attempt, switch to notebook mode or review a submitted paper |
| Review | Explain what will appear, never show fictitious metrics | Wrong, partial, slow or guessed-right answers; retry without seeing the old answer |
| Library | Browse syllabus, papers by stream/year/format, sources and formula sheets | Restore filters and scroll position; open or share a permanent question URL |

### Learn before testing

Map the official syllabus to a teaching order, with a visible distinction between open, planned and missing lessons. Teach via prediction → explanation → worked example → fading hints → independent variant → misconception feedback → revision → PYQ. In maths show why a step is valid; in physics label signs and units; in chemistry explain tempting distractors; in electrical draw the circuit with labelled nodes. Revision sheets state assumptions, units and a counterexample, not formulas alone.

Pilot **parallel circuits**: two 6 Ω branches across an ideal 12 V source each carry 2 A. Open a branch: the other still has 12 V across it. Ask the learner to predict before showing the drawing change; retry a different case later. Test transfer, not page views. If no matching verified PYQ is found, use a labelled original exercise instead. Never imply the ten-item exam audit is a ten-question set about circuits.

Add reviewed Bengali intuition alongside English terminology where useful; preserve the official English stem. Choose videos for a specific learning objective, with language, timestamp, length/segment and why to watch. Tap to open; provide a text and diagram alternative. Never host third-party video or depend on a playlist or YouTube API.

### Practice, review and planning

- Guided: hint ladder with immediate reasoning. Independent drill: choose topic/category/set size. Label assistance; opening a hint is not independent success. Notebook mode: show one problem, hide steps until requested; backgrounding the tab preserves place.
- Reader: all publishable records, including unscored items. Rehearsal: time limit, navigator, no hints; clearly separate editable choices from optional irreversible OMR ink. Printed-paper companion: select year, paper and set before recording responses.
- After submission show response → key and source → mark calculation → subject reasoning → precise lesson section. Distinguish first try from retry. Preserve original attempt when repeating a set.
- Offer voluntary reason tags (concept, method, calculation, misread). Use retry dates and independent outcomes for revision, not punishment. Coverage, independent results with sample size, and items due are separate signals. Opening a page ≠ mastery. Time-budget plan example: one lesson, two problems, one retry; a missed day reschedules unfinished work.
- AI assistance at launch means reviewed, prewritten hints. Optional copyable prompt previews only the chosen content; never sends notes or attempts automatically. No AI help inside a timed paper. Live tutoring is conditional, not necessary for any journey.
