# Web UI reference and deep-link contract

The source review of the owner's [Diploma Study Hub and related semester project](UI_REFRESH_RESEARCH.md), and the scoped Today-dashboard refresh, are recorded separately. They inform interaction patterns only; JELET's product, content, theme and exam rules remain authoritative here.

Study the [Diploma Study Hub](https://github.com/pabitra27706-oss/Diploma-3rd-sem/tree/06656a9c3166ed86f0d3e05e965dfda068ec5a6e) for behaviour, not code, assets, styling or exam rules. Source reviewed; no visual/device pass is claimed. Use its Today focus, conditional Continue, exact-question share, local settings and [explicit topic routing](https://github.com/pabitra27706-oss/Diploma-3rd-sem/blob/06656a9c3166ed86f0d3e05e965dfda068ec5a6e/docs/phases/topic-map-standard.md).

**Reference journey:** open a shared PYQ on a fresh phone → see its year, paper, booklet set, number, category, marks and source → Learn this concept jumps to the reviewed *section anchor* → Back restores the same question and tentative choices. The lesson lists all questions reviewed against that section. Multiple concepts may be linked with one teaching target marked primary. No confirmed match: keep the PYQ permalink, say “Lesson not mapped yet”; never guess silently. Old slugs/aliases should resolve after a title edit. Validate both anchor existence and teaching relevance.

**Sharing:** Android share sheet when available; clipboard then selectable text fallback. Share question only (no spoiler) by default; explicit “with solution” link only when a solution exists. Share metadata and canonical URL, not a screenshot of a private attempt. Recipient needs no account or session storage. In rehearsal hide solution links until submission.

**Search:** group lessons, PYQs and revision resources. Filter by subject/topic, year, stream, category and readiness. Empty state explains how to broaden the query. “Find a question from a photo” guides the student to use Google Lens outside our site, copy a distinctive line and paste it into local search. No direct Lens API, automatic upload or answer certification. Normalise whitespace and option prefixes, preserve meaningful signs/exponents, show candidates with year and number for confirmation. Offer manual filters for diagrams, OCR errors or no match. Allow manual entry without Google.

**Design:** five destinations as in [Product](PRODUCT.md). Paper-like reading surface, dark ink, one primary action, text labels for subject/status. On mobile, enlarge a diagram and dismiss it without losing place. Let a long equation scroll inside its container, not the whole page. On desktop use a bounded reading column and optional contents/navigator. Support visible keyboard focus, accessible figure descriptions, labelled controls, generous touch targets, non-colour-only grading, dark mode and reduced motion. Exam mode must stay quiet. Check loading, empty, missing source, denied storage, offline and interrupted resume states, not just the happy path.
