### Q31 — relative permeability

**Viewer page 8; printed page 8. Diagram: none. Stored comparison: ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.**

Printed data: iron ring, mean circumference 140 cm, cross-section 12 cm², 500 turns, current 2 A, flux 1.2 mWb. Options: A 1114 T; B 1114; C 800; D 3000.

**Derived: B — 1114.** Area=0.0012 m² and flux=0.0012 Wb give B_field=1 T. H=NI/l=1000/1.4=714.286 A/m. Thus μ_r=B_field/(μ₀H)=1114.085, dimensionless. A's tesla unit makes it incorrect even though its number matches.

**Assumptions:** uniform ring flux, negligible leakage, mean magnetic path as printed. **Fallback comparison:** stem/options match mathematically.
