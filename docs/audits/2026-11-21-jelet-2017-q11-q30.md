### Q11 — exact differential equation

**Viewer page 4; printed page 4. Diagram: none.**

Printed expression: `(cos y + y cos x) dx + (sin x − x sin y) dy`, followed by “is”; the scan does **not explicitly print =0**. Options:

- A: x cos y + y sin x = c
- B: x sin y + y cos x = c
- C: x cos y − y sin x = c
- D: x sin y − y cos x = c

**Proposed answer: A — x cos y + y sin x = c.** Under the conventional interpretation that the displayed differential expression equals zero, its potential is Φ=x cos y+y sin x. Direct differentiation gives Φ_x=cos y+y cos x and Φ_y=sin x−x sin y, exactly the two printed coefficients. Thus dΦ=0 implies Φ=c.

**Assumption:** omitted RHS is zero; do not present that as printed ink. **Transcription:** options and coefficients match; the archived fallback explicitly adds `=0`, an inferred completion rather than a verbatim feature of the scan. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.

### Q12 — complementary function: two valid bases

**Viewer page 5; printed page 5. Diagram: none.**

Printed stem: complementary function of `(D²+4)y=2x+3`. Options:

- A: (A+Bx)e^(±ix)
- B: A e^(i2x)+B e^(−i2x)
- C: A cosh x+B sinh x
- D: A cos 2x+B sin 2x

**Source-only verdict: ANSWER-AMBIGUOUS. Both B and D represent the complementary function under the usual allowance of complex exponential constants.** The homogeneous characteristic equation m²+4=0 has roots ±2i. Therefore y_h=C₁e^(2ix)+C₂e^(−2ix), equivalently y_h=C₃ cos 2x+C₄ sin 2x, by Euler's identities and a change of arbitrary constants.

For real-valued solutions, D with real constants is the conventional form; B describes the same real family when its coefficients are constrained as complex conjugates. The printed question does not specify real constants only or require a trigonometric form. Do not force D as the unique mathematically valid printed option without acknowledging B. This is **not** a multi-select answer proposal for a single-choice paper; it is a non-uniqueness hold.

**Transcription:** stem and all options agree mathematically; ambiguity is present in the scan itself. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE; the imported record is unavailable, so no assertion about its choice is made.

### Q14 — homogeneous function

**Viewer page 5; printed page 5. Diagram: none.**

### Q15 — implicit partial derivatives

**Viewer page 5; printed page 5. Diagram: none.**

Printed data: F(v²−x²,v²−y²,v²−z²)=0, v=v(x,y,z). Find v_x/x+v_y/y+v_z/z. Options: A 1/x; B 1/y; C 1/z; D 1/v.

**Proposed answer: D — 1/v, on a regular branch.** Let F₁,F₂,F₃ denote derivatives with respect to the three arguments and S=F₁+F₂+F₃. Differentiating with respect to x gives 2vS v_x−2xF₁=0, hence v_x/x=F₁/(vS). The y and z expressions similarly give F₂/(vS) and F₃/(vS); their sum is S/(vS)=1/v.

**Assumptions:** F and v differentiable; x,y,z,v are nonzero where the displayed quotients are evaluated; vS≠0 so the implicit relation locally determines v by the usual regularity condition. The question does not state these qualifications; singular or vacuous F does not justify division by S. **Transcription:** no mathematical mismatch. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.

### Q18 — continuous density normalisation

**Viewer page 6; printed page 6. Diagram: none.** The piecewise function was enlarged for inspection.

Printed distribution: f(x)=k x² e^(−x/2) for x>0, and 0 elsewhere. Find k. Options: A 1/10; B 1/12; C 3/14; D 1/16.

**Proposed answer: D — 1/16.** Normalisation requires 1=k∫₀∞x²e^(−x/2)dx. Substitution t=x/2 gives the integral 8∫₀∞t²e^(−t)dt=8·2!=16, so k=1/16. This positive k makes f nonnegative and normalised.

**Assumption:** the printed “probability distribution” is interpreted as a density, as its continuous piecewise expression requires. **Transcription mismatch:** fallback replaces the density with an entirely different discrete table at x=1,2,3,4 with probabilities k,2k,3k,4k. This is a major answer-affecting stem mismatch, not a minor missing symbol. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE; no claim is made that an imported record actually selects A.

### Q22 — frequency from angular frequency

**Viewer page 6; printed page 6. Diagram: none.**

### Q23 — nearly resonant series RLC circuit

**Viewer page 7; printed page 7. Diagram: none.**

Printed data: R=20 Ω, C=100 μF, L=0.1 H, f=50 Hz. Options: A nearly 0° lagging; B nearly 0° leading; C 0°; D 90° leading.

**Proposed answer: B — nearly 0° leading.** X_L=2πfL=31.41593 Ω; X_C=1/(2πfC)=31.83099 Ω; net reactance X=−0.41506 Ω is capacitive. The current phase relative to voltage is −atan(X/R)=+1.18889°. Thus the current slightly leads; exact-zero phase (C) would incorrectly discard the residual reactance.

**Assumptions:** ideal lumped components with the printed values and usual sinusoidal steady state. Keep adequate precision to distinguish leading from lagging. **Transcription:** no mathematical mismatch. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.

### Q24 — dimension of inductance

**Viewer page 7; printed page 7. Diagram: none.** Option equations enlarged for inspection.

Printed options for the equation from which to find inductance's dimension:

- A: v=iR
- B: e=−L di/dt
- C: i=c dv/dt
- D: E=(1/2)mv²

**Proposed answer: B — e=−L di/dt.** Rearranging dimensionally, [L]=[e][t]/[i]=M L_length² T^(−2) I^(−2), the dimension of the henry. The minus sign specifies the induced-emf direction and has no effect on dimensions. A determines resistance, C capacitance, and D energy, not inductance.

**Assumption:** usual meanings of e, i, t and the other printed symbols. **Transcription mismatch:** fallback B is `v=L di/dt`, not the printed induced-emf form `e=−L di/dt`; the dimensional result happens to agree. Fallback D is `v=(1/C)∫i dt`, a capacitor-voltage equation, whereas the scan prints **E=(1/2)mv²**. A and C agree mathematically (capitalisation of capacitance aside). Do not preserve an invented distractor merely because the selected label is unchanged. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.

### Q25 — motor-overload relay

**Viewer page 7; printed page 7. Diagram: none.**

Printed question: relay used for motor protection against overload. Options: A differential relay; B Buchholz relay; C induction relay; D thermal relay.

**Proposed answer: D — thermal relay.** Sustained motor overload raises winding temperature through I²R heating. A thermal overload relay models this heating and trips after an appropriate delay, rather than treating ordinary starting current as an immediate fault. Buchholz protection applies to oil-filled transformer faults; differential protection is not the conventional overload choice here.

**Assumption:** standard basic motor-protection context; this is not a claim that every modern motor-protection implementation must be thermal. **Transcription:** no mathematical mismatch. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.

### Q29 — starting back emf

**Viewer page 7; printed page 7. Diagram: none.**

Printed stem: back emf of a DC shunt motor at starting. Options: A zero; B equal to supply voltage; C infinity; D about 50% of supply voltage.

**Proposed answer: A — zero.** Rotational back emf E_b=kΦω. At the initial standstill, ω=0, so E_b=0 even if field flux is present; this is why starting armature current needs limitation.

**Assumption:** “at starting” means the instant before rotation, and “back emf” means rotational emf, not an inductive switching transient. **Transcription:** no mathematical mismatch. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.

### Q30 — delta-to-star conversion

**Viewer page 8; printed page 8. Diagram: none.**

Printed data: three equal 12 Ω resistances connected in delta; find resistance in each branch of equivalent star. Options: A 4 ohm; B 8 ohm; C 16 ohm; D 10 ohm.

**Proposed answer: A — 4 ohm.** Each star arm is the product of its adjacent delta resistances divided by the sum of all delta resistances: R_Y=(12×12)/(12+12+12)=4 Ω. As a check, between any two external terminals the star gives 4+4=8 Ω, equal to the delta's 12||(12+12)=8 Ω.

**Assumption:** equivalent terminal network of ideal resistors; “branch” means one star arm, not the two-terminal resistance of the full network. **Transcription:** no mathematical mismatch. **Stored comparison:** ANSWER-NOT-CHECKABLE.
